# CHALO FARVA — SUPPLIER PORTAL OPERATIONS GUIDE v1.0

**Product**: Chalo Farva (Gujarat-First AI Travel Platform)  
**Version**: `v1.0.0` (Build `v1.26.0`)  
**Target Audience**: Verified Travel Suppliers & Marketplace Partners

---

## 1. INVENTORY & AVAILABILITY MANAGEMENT

1. Log into `Supplier Portal` -> `Service Management`.
2. Select target service (e.g., *Fern Sardar Sarovar Resort Kevadia*).
3. Click `Add Inventory / Room Type`. Define room title, capacity (e.g. 2 adults), and base price.
4. Set availability calendar blocks.
5. Click `PUBLISH INVENTORY`.

---

## 2. SETTLEMENT & PAYOUT TRACKING

1. Log into `Supplier Portal` -> `Financials & Settlements`.
2. View weekly settlement statements generated every Monday.
3. Inspect gross bookings, platform commission (10%), 1% TDS deduction under Sec 194O, and net payable.
4. Download PDF/CSV settlement statements for tax accounting.
