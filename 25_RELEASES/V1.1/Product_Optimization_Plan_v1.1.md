# Product Optimization Plan v1.1 — Chalo Farva

**Scope**: Data-Driven Product Enhancements targeting AI Acceptance & Checkout Progression  

---

## Priority Optimization Initiatives

### 1. AI Acceptance Optimization (Stage 5 → Stage 6)
- **Problem**: 28.0% of generated itineraries are dropped or edited due to long-distance travel fatigue between distant Gujarat hubs (e.g. Statue of Unity to Dwarka).
- **Optimization Solution**: Introduce mandatory intermediate hub stopovers (Vadodara/Rajkot break points) and sticky 1-click itinerary accept CTA (`EXP-001`).
- **Target KPI**: Increase AI Itinerary Acceptance Rate from 72.0% to **$\ge 78.0\%$**.

### 2. Checkout Progression Optimization (Stage 6 → Stage 7)
- **Problem**: 21.2% checkout drop-off caused by mobile form complexity during passenger/guest detail entry.
- **Optimization Solution**: Implement auto-fill for logged-in user profile details, clear budget summary modal (`EXP-003`), and mobile quick-select passenger toggles.
- **Target KPI**: Increase Checkout to Payment conversion from 78.8% to **$\ge 85.0\%$**.

### 3. Adaptive AI Transparency & User Trust (Stage 8 → Stage 9)
- **Problem**: Travelers demand clear explanations before approving weather disruption re-routings.
- **Optimization Solution**: Roll out in-app glassmorphic interactive weather delay toasts (`EXP-002`) with zero autonomous financial charges.
- **Target KPI**: Maintain 0 unapproved charges while increasing adaptation approval rate to **$\ge 88.0\%$**.
