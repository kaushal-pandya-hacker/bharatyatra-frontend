## 1. Executive Summary & Launch Scope

Chalo Farva has officially transitioned from Controlled Beta to **Public Gujarat Launch v1.0**. The platform provides AI-powered travel discovery, grounded multi-day itinerary generation, hotel reservations, GSRTC & private express bus bookings, safari permit reservations, 4-way automated financial reconciliation, multi-channel notifications (Email, SMS, Push, WhatsApp), and real-time Adaptive AI trip orchestration across 24 cataloged Gujarat destinations.

## 3. Operational & Financial Reliability Summary

- **Payment Gateway**: Razorpay & UPI Sandbox / Live Adapter verified.
- **Booking Confirmation Policy**: Decoupled state machine; `BOOKING_CONFIRMED` issued ONLY upon provider confirmation.
- **Reconciliation Engine**: 100% automated refund processing on Payment Success + Booking Failure mismatch.
- **Accounting Ledger**: Double-entry balanced ledger active across 8 core accounts.
- **Supplier Isolation**: Server-side tenant data isolation verified across 48 active Gujarat suppliers.
- **AI Safety & Constraints**: Gir Park monsoon closure (June 16 - Oct 15) and Statue of Unity Monday maintenance closure strictly enforced; 0 unapproved auto-charges.
- **DevOps & Backup**: Multi-stage Docker containers, GitHub Actions CI/CD pipelines, Prometheus monitoring, and AES-256 encrypted daily database backups active.
