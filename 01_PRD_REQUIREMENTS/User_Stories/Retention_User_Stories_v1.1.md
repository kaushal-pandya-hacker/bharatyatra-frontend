# Retention User Stories v1.1

**Project**: Chalo Farva  
**Phase**: Post-Launch Optimization & Growth v1.1  
**Version**: v1.25.0  
**Date**: September 13, 2026  

---

## User Stories Inventory

### US-RET-001: My Trip Long-Term Travel Archive
- **As a** returning Chalo Farva customer,
- **I want to** view all my past itineraries, upcoming vouchers, and saved trip ideas in one central dashboard,
- **So that** I can easily re-book or extend previous travel plans without re-entering details from scratch.

### US-RET-002: Organic AI Repeat Trip Suggestions
- **As a** user who completed a weekend heritage trip to Ahmedabad,
- **I want to** receive personalized, non-invasive recommendations for a subsequent trip (e.g. Statue of Unity or Champaner),
- **So that** I can discover complementary travel destinations across Gujarat.

### US-RET-003: Post-Trip Summary & 1-Click Review
- **As a** traveler who just finished a 3-day Saurashtra circuit,
- **I want to** receive a clean summary card of my trip and share feedback on my hotel and bus experience,
- **So that** my feedback helps improve supplier quality and my past itinerary is preserved in My Trip.

### US-RET-004: Ethical Transparent Upsell & Add-on
- **As a** customer booking a hotel stay in Dwarka,
- **I want to** see optional transit or temple VIP pass add-ons with clear incremental pricing,
- **So that** I can customize my trip without hidden fees or pre-checked surprise charges.
