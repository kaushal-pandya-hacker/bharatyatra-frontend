# Configurable GST & Tax Structure v1.0

## 1. Tax Classification (India GST Standards)
- **Hotel Stays**: 12% GST (6% CGST + 6% SGST) for room rate < ₹7,500/night; 18% for >= ₹7,500/night.
- **Bus Transport**: 5% GST (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST).
- **Platform Convenience Fees**: 18% GST (9% CGST + 9% SGST).

## 2. Tax Computation Rule
- All taxes are calculated on net taxable subtotal after applying eligible discounts.
