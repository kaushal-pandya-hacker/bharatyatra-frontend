## 1. Brand Identity & Overview

- **Brand Name**: Chalo Farva (ચાલો ફરવા)
- **Tagline**: *"Plan less. Coordinate less. Enjoy more."*
- **Meaning**: "Chalo Farva" is Gujarati for *"Let's go explore / travel"*. It reflects warmth, spontaneous exploration, and effortless travel planning across Gujarat and beyond.
- **Brand Core Position**: Not just a booking site, but an **adaptive travel companion and live trip-management intelligence**.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Visual Tone

Chalo Farva uses a vibrant, modern Gujarati heritage palette combined with dark mode & glassmorphism accents for high digital visual impact.

### Primary Palette
- **Rann Sunset Orange (`#FF6B35`)**: Symbolizes hospitality, vibrant culture, and energy.
- **Gir Royal Teal (`#008080`)**: Represents nature, wildlife, reliability, and tranquility.
- **Somnath Deep Navy (`#0A192F`)**: Foundation background for sleek dark mode interfaces.

### Accent & Neutral Palette
- **Saurashtra Gold (`#FFC107`)**: Highlights, badges, top ratings, and CTA accents.
- **Kutch Soft Sand (`#F4F1EA`)**: Warm light mode canvas background.
- **Glass Slate (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`)**: Backdrop blur overlays for modern UI cards.

---

## 3. Typography Direction

- **Primary Heading Font**: **Outfit** / **Plus Jakarta Sans** (Google Fonts) — Modern, clean geometric sans-serif for strong headings.
- **Body Font**: **Inter** (Google Fonts) — Highly readable across desktop and mobile screens.
- **Gujarati Script Font**: **Noto Sans Gujarati** — For authentic regional script rendering.

### Placement Guidelines
1. **Clear Space**: Maintain a minimum clear space surrounding the logo equal to 50% of the logo mark height.
2. **Background Context**: Use the white/transparent vector variant on dark surfaces (`#0A192F`) and full-color logo mark on light sand (`#F4F1EA`).
3. **Prohibited Alterations**: Do not stretch, skew, change element colors, or alter font aspect ratios of the approved brand mark.

## 5. Brand Voice & Communication Tone

- **Welcoming & Helpful**: Uses warm, encouraging language ("Kem Chho! Ready for your next journey?").
- **Reliable & Precise**: Gives accurate, verified timing, distance, and booking pricing.
- **Adaptive & Reassuring**: When plans change due to weather or delays, the voice stays calm and solution-oriented ("Rain expected in Somnath. Here is a seamless indoor museum alternative!").
