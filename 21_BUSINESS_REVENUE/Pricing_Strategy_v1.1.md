---

## 1. Overview & Transparent Pricing Policy

The Chalo Farva Pricing Strategy enforces **100% Price Transparency**.

### Core Pricing Principles
1. **Zero Hidden Fees**: The price displayed on the search result card matches the final payment amount at checkout.
2. **Itemized Fee Disclosure**: Room rate, transit fare, entry tickets, platform fee (₹49 flat), and GST (18%) are itemized explicitly.
3. **No Unannounced Price Inflation**: Price modifications by suppliers during checkout trigger mandatory user re-confirmation.

---

## 2. Platform Revenue & Commission Structure

- **Hotels**: 12.0% – 15.0% commission on room rate.
- **Bus Operators**: 8.0% – 10.0% commission on seat fare.
- **Activity Providers**: 15.0% – 20.0% commission on ticket entry.
- **Travel Packages**: 14.0% – 18.0% margin on complete package price.
- **Platform Booking Fee**: Flat ₹49.00 per completed booking transaction.
