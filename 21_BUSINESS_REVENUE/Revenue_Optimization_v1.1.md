## 2. Ethical Cross-Selling & Upselling Rules

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| User Journey Trigger    | Context-Aware Recommendation    | Value Proposition     |
+-------------------------+---------------------------------+-----------------------+
| Hotel Checkout Completed| "Add Bus / Cab Transit"         | 1-Click seamless route|
| Bus Booking Completed   | "Add Near-Station Hotel Stay"   | Verified 4+ star stay |
| Single Hotel Booking    | "Upgrade to Full Package (+20%)"| Includes SOU VIP pass |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Strict Guardrails
> [!CAUTION]
> 1. **No Hidden Charges**: All cross-sell and upsell options explicitly display the breakdown: `Original Price + Additional Item Cost = Final Total`.
> 2. **No Pre-Ticked Checkboxes**: Pre-selecting add-ons or insurance checkboxes during checkout is strictly prohibited.
