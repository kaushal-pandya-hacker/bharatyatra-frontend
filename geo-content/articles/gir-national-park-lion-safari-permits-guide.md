## 1. Introduction: The Last Bastion of the Asiatic Lion

Spanning 1,412 square kilometers across Junagadh, Gir Somnath, and Amreli districts in Saurashtra, **Gir National Park and Wildlife Sanctuary** is the sole surviving natural habitat of the Asiatic Lion (*Panthera leo persica*). According to official wildlife census data from the Gujarat Forest Department, the wild lion population across the Greater Gir landscape stands at over 674 individuals.

For wildlife photographers and travelers, securing a safari in Gir is a bucket-list ambition. However, Gir operates under strict statutory quotas, mandatory identity matching, and an immovable seasonal closure calendar.

## 2. The Annual Monsoon Closure: Legal Dates & Buffer Alternatives

The most critical operational rule governing Gir is its statutory monsoon closure:

> [!WARNING]
> **Official Forest Department Gazette Rule:**  
> The core **Gir Jungle Safari (Gir Jungle Trail)** is strictly **CLOSED TO VISITORS every year from June 16 through October 15**.

### 2.1 Why the Core Sanctuary Closes
1. **Breeding Season:** The monsoon coincides with the biological mating and cub-rearing cycle of the Asiatic lion. Human and vehicular disruption is prohibited under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972.
2. **Terrain Inundation:** The forest tracks crossing the Hiran, Machhundri, Raval, and Shingoda rivers become washed out and impassable for four-wheel-drive safari vehicles.

### 2.2 The All-Season Sanctuary Alternative: Devalia Safari Park
Travelers visiting Saurashtra between June 16 and October 15 can still experience Asiatic lions in their natural habitat at **Devalia Safari Park (Gir Interpretation Zone)**:
- **Location:** 12 km west of Sasan Gir along the Mendarda Road.
- **Operating Calendar:** Open year-round **except every Wednesday**.
- **Safari Modalities:** Mini-bus safaris (operated by the Forest Department) or open-top registered Gypsies.
- **Sightings:** 412 hectares of fenced deciduous thorn scrub containing pure-bred Asiatic lions, leopards, spotted deer (chital), sambar, and blackbucks.
- **Alternative Monsoon Eco-Facility:** **Ambardi Safari Park** near Dhari (Amreli district) also operates year-round as a satellite eco-tourism sanctuary.

## 3. Official Permit Booking Mechanics: Step-by-Step

Many travelers fall victim to unverified third-party booking websites that mimic official government domains, charging markups of 300% to 500%.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Single Authorized Booking Gateway:**  
> The **ONLY** legitimate platform authorized to issue Gir safari entry permits is the Gujarat Forest Department portal:  
> **`https://girlion.gujarat.gov.in`**

### 3.1 Advance Booking Windows & Quotas
- **Booking Window:** Permits open exactly **90 days in advance** at 00:00 IST on the government portal.
- **Peak Dates:** Slots for Diwali week, Christmas through New Year (Dec 22 – Jan 5), and summer holidays (April–May) routinely sell out within minutes of release.
- **Daily Shifts (Gir Jungle Trail):**
  1. Morning Shift 1: 06:00 to 09:00 (Best for territorial lion patrols in summer)
  2. Morning Shift 2: 09:00 to 12:00
  3. Evening Shift 3: 15:00 to 18:00 (Best for golden hour photography and watering hole activity)

### 3.2 Vehicle Capacity & Photo ID Matching Rules
- **Maximum Passengers:** Each Gypsy entry permit accommodates a maximum of **6 adults and 1 child (between 3 and 12 years old)**.
- **Strict Photo ID Rule:** The primary applicant must input exact government photo identification details for every passenger:
  - Indian Citizens: Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License.
  - Foreign Nationals: Valid Passport number and Nationality.
- **Zero-Tolerance Gate Check:** At the Sinh Sadan entry gate in Sasan Gir, Forest Department wardens cross-examine the physical original IDs against the printed e-permit. Any discrepancy in name spelling, ID number, or identity card type results in **immediate permit cancellation without refund**. No substitutes or additions are permitted on the day of travel.

## 5. Transit Realities & Reaching Sasan Gir

- **Headquarters Coordinates:** Sinh Sadan Reception Center, Sasan Gir (Junagadh District, Gujarat).
- **By Road from Ahmedabad:** 360 km via NH 47 to Rajkot, then NH 151 through Junagadh. Travel time: **7 to 8 hours**.
- **By Road from Somnath:** Exactly **45 km north** along the Veraval–Sasan road. Travel time: **1 hour 15 minutes**.
- **By Road from Rajkot (Nearest Commercial Airport):** 155 km via Junagadh. Travel time: **3.5 hours**.
- **By Train:** Sasan Gir railway station (SASN) has regional passenger train connections. The primary broad-gauge junction is **Junagadh Junction (JND)**, 55 km away.
