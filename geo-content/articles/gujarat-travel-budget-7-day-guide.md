- **Day 1: Ahmedabad Urban Heritage:** Sabarmati Ashram, Adalaj Stepwell, Sidi Saiyyed Mosque, Old City Heritage Walk, Manek Chowk street cuisine.
- **Day 2: Statue of Unity (Ekta Nagar):** High-speed transit via NE 1 / Jan Shatabdi Express, Viewing Gallery, Valley of Flowers, evening projection mapping show. Overnight Vadodara/Ekta Nagar.
- **Day 3: Vadodara to Sasan Gir:** Highway transit across central Gujarat (NH 47 to NH 151), check-in near Hiran River, evening Devalia Safari Park buffer zone visit.
- **Day 4: Sasan Gir Wilderness to Somnath:** Early morning Asiatic Lion Jungle Safari (06:00–09:00), lunch, afternoon transit to Prabhas Patan (45 km), evening aarti at Shree Somnath Jyotirlinga and Sound & Light Show.
- **Day 5: Somnath to Dwarka Coastal Drive (NH 51):** 235 km coastal drive via Madhavpur Turtle Hatchery, Porbandar Kirti Mandir (Gandhi's birthplace), and evening darshan at Dwarkadhish Temple.
- **Day 6: Dwarka, Bet Dwarka & Nageshwar:** Sudarshan Setu cable bridge crossing, Bet Dwarka darshan, Nageshwar Jyotirlinga, Rukmini Devi Temple, sunset at Shivrajpur Blue Flag Beach.
- **Day 7: Dwarka to Ahmedabad / Rajkot Return:** Morning checkout, transit via NH 27 (via Jamnagar & Rajkot), return flight/train departure from Ahmedabad Junction or Rajkot Hirasar Airport.

#### Private Hired Vehicle Economics
If hiring an air-conditioned car with a commercial driver across Gujarat, insist on a written contract specifying:
- **Base Rate:** Sedan (Dzire/Etios) @ ₹11–₹13/km; 6-seater SUV (Ertiga) @ ₹14–₹16/km; Premium SUV (Innova Crysta) @ ₹18–₹22/km.
- **Minimum Distance Clause:** Standard commercial operators enforce a mandatory minimum of **250 km to 300 km per day**. For a 7-day trip, you will be billed for a minimum of 1,750 km even if your actual odometer reads 1,550 km.
- **Driver Night Allowance (*Bhatta*):** Standard rate is **₹300 to ₹500 per calendar night** if the driver sleeps away from his home base.
- **Highway Tolls & Parking:** Fastag tolls along the NH 47, NE 1, NH 151, and NH 27 circuit average **₹1,450 to ₹1,650**. Parking fees across monuments and temple lots add ₹250 to ₹400.

Gujarat is globally renowned for its vegetarian culinary heritage. Meals are affordable, wholesome, and widely available along every state highway:

- **Unlimited Authentic Kathiyawadi Thali:** ₹150 to ₹250 per person at highway hubs like *Grand Thakar*, *Gordhan Thal*, *Honest*, and *Patel Dining Hall*. - **Strict Jain & Swaminarayan Dining:** 100% onion-and-garlic-free (*no kandmool*) meals are available at identical tariff rates across all major pilgrimage corridors without premium surcharges.

## 5. Hidden Expenses, Traps & The Emergency Buffer

Travelers planning an Indian road trip often fail to account for minor recurring expenses that silently drain 15% to 20% of their total cash pool:

1. **Driver Intercity Bhatta & Overtime:** If your driver operates past 22:00 IST or stays overnight outside his home city, an extra night charge of **₹300 to ₹500** is mandatory. Ensure your hotel has free driver dormitory facilities; otherwise, budget an extra ₹300 for commercial driver rest pods.
2. **Mandatory Temple Outer Lockers & Shoe Counters:** Phones and smartwatches are forbidden inside Somnath and Dwarkadhish. While official lockers are free, shoe-attendants at temple ghats and private counters charge ₹10 to ₹20 per pair.
3. **Safari Gypsy Gratuities & DSLR Cameras:** In Gir National Park, bringing a professional DSLR camera with a lens exceeding 70mm incurs a mandatory state fee of **₹200 per camera**. Eco-guides typically anticipate a voluntary gratuity of ₹200 to ₹300 per trip.
4. **Bottled Drinking Water & Hydration:** Saurashtra’s dry heat requires high water intake. Budget **₹60 to ₹100 per day** for sealed ISI-certified mineral water bottles (₹20 each) or carry stainless steel bottles for RO refilling at trust hotels.
5. **The Recommended 10% Emergency Contingency Fund:** Maintain a liquid buffer of **₹3,000 for solo travelers, ₹6,000 for couples, and ₹10,000 for families** in an active UPI account or cash for vehicle puncture repairs, pharmacy medicines, or unexpected road diversions.

## 6. Practical Money-Saving Strategies for Gujarat

- **Tip 1: Book Gir Safaris 90 Days in Advance Online:** Never purchase last-minute Gir safari packages from roadside touts charging ₹12,000–₹16,000. Book directly on `girlion.gujarat.gov.in` 90 days ahead at 00:00 IST for statutory state fees (₹3,500–₹4,300 total for up to 6 people).
- **Tip 2: Use Sudarshan Setu Instead of Ferry Boats:** Avoid tourist boat touts at Okha port claiming ferry tickets are required for Bet Dwarka. The 2.32 km Sudarshan Setu cable bridge is completely open, toll-free, and accessible by car, auto, or bus 24 hours a day.
- **Tip 3: Book ASI Monument Tickets via QR Code:** Scanning the official ASI QR boards at Rani ki Vav, Modhera, and Adalaj yields an instant 10% to 20% discount compared to cash counter queues.
- **Tip 4: Travel Mid-Week for Accommodation:** Hotel tariffs in Sasan Gir and SOU fluctuate by up to 40% between Tuesday–Thursday versus Saturday–Sunday.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the minimum realistic budget for a 7-day trip to Gujarat?
A solo backpacker utilizing GSRTC buses, sleeper trains, and temple trust dharamshalas can comfortably complete a 7-day Gujarat circuit for **₹10,500 to ₹14,200 total** (~₹1,500 to ₹2,000 per day), including all food, entry fees, and shared transit.

### How much does an AC car with a driver cost for 7 days in Gujarat?
A dedicated commercial sedan (Swift Dzire or Toyota Etios) costs between **₹21,000 and ₹26,000 total** for 7 days. This includes the mandatory 250 km daily minimum allowance (1,750 km billed), fuel, driver night allowances (*bhatta*), and highway Fastag tolls.

### Does a family of 4 need to book a full tour package?
No. Independent travel in Gujarat is exceptionally safe, straightforward, and affordable. Booking your hotels directly, hiring a dedicated Ertiga SUV, and reserving official monument tickets on government portals saves a family of four between **₹30,000 and ₹65,000** compared to commercial third-party holiday packages.

### Are temple trust guesthouses in Somnath and Dwarka clean and reliable?
Yes. Shree Somnath Trust guesthouses (such as Sagar Darshan, Lilavati Bhavan, and Maheshwari Bhavan) and verified dharamshalas in Dwarka offer air-conditioned, elevator-equipped, spotless rooms ranging from ₹800 to ₹2,500 per night. They must be booked through the official trust portal (`somnath.org`) or registered pilgrim directories.

### How much should I budget for meals per day in Gujarat?
Authentic Gujarati and Kathiyawadi unlimited thalis cost between **₹150 and ₹250 per person** at highway family dhabas and city restaurants. A realistic daily food budget is **₹400 to ₹500 per person** for budget travelers and **₹800 to ₹1,200 per person** for those dining at heritage restaurants and hotel buffets.
