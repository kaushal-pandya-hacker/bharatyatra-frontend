# Verification Process & Provenance Badging — Chalo Farva

## Provenance Badges
1. `VERIFIED_DATA`: Checked against official TCGL, ASI, or Forest Department documentation.
2. `LIVE_PROVIDER_DATA`: Fetched dynamically via bus/hotel supplier API.
3. `USER_GENERATED`: Traveler reviews and ratings.
4. `AI_GENERATED_RECOMMENDATION`: Generative AI suggested itineraries requiring rule validation.
