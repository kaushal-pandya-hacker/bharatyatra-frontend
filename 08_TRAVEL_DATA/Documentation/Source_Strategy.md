# Source & Freshness Strategy — Chalo Farva

## Official Sources Tiering
- **Tier 1 (High Authority)**: Tourism Corporation of Gujarat Limited (TCGL), Archaeological Survey of India (ASI), Gujarat Forest Department, Statue of Unity Authority.
- **Tier 2 (Official Partners)**: GSRTC, District Collectorates.
- **Tier 3 (Verified Operators)**: Certified local tour guides and resort operators.

## Freshness Audit Intervals
- Opening Hours & Ticket Fees: Audited every 60 days.
- Festival Dates: Audited annually in August.
- Weather & Monsoon Closures: Audited weekly during monsoon season (June - October).
