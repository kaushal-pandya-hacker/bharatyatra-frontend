# Weather Adaptation Strategy v1.0

## 1. Supported Triggers
- `RAIN_ALERT`, `HEAVY_RAIN_WARNING`
- `HEAT_ALERT` (> 42°C in Gujarat summer)
- `WIND_WARNING`, `POOR_VISIBILITY`

## 2. Adaptation Behavior
- **Rain Warning**: Automatically swaps outdoor activities (beaches, gardens, open heritage walks) to nearby indoor galleries, museums, or science parks.
- **Heat Warning**: Shifts strenuous outdoor sightseeing to early morning (07:00 AM) or evening (06:30 PM), inserting air-conditioned indoor slots during peak heat (12:00 PM – 04:00 PM).
