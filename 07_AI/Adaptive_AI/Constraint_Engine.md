# Hard & Soft Constraint Engine v1.0

## 1. Hard Non-Negotiable Constraints
1. **Financial Guardrail**: Zero automatic payments or unapproved charges.
2. **Paid Booking Guardrail**: Never cancel or rebook paid tickets (hotel/bus) without explicit user confirmation.
3. **Opening Hours**: Replacements must fit verified venue operating hours.
4. **Monsoon Closures**: Respect annual breeding season closures (Gir Park June 16 - Oct 15).
5. **Closed Days**: Respect Monday closures (Statue of Unity).

## 2. Soft Optimization Constraints
- User travel pace preference (`relaxed` | `balanced` | `fast_paced`).
- Minimize travel distance detour.
- Prefer indoor cultural/heritage alternatives during rain.
- Preserve overall trip theme & user interests.
