# Deterministic Hard Constraint Engine v1.0

## 1. Responsibilities
The `HardConstraintEngine` enforces non-negotiable operational rules for travel in Gujarat.

## 2. Rule Specifications
- **Monsoon Closures**:
  - `dest-gir-national-park` (Gir National Park): CLOSED annually from **June 16 to October 15** for wildlife breeding season.
- **Closed Days**:
  - `dest-statue-of-unity` (Statue of Unity): CLOSED every **Monday** for weekly maintenance.
- **Daily Distance Limits**:
  - Maximum travel distance per day: **350 km**.
- **Opening Hours**:
  - Activity time slots must fit within verified POI operating hours.
