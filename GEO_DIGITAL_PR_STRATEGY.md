## 1. Ethical Digital PR Principles & Policy

Search engine generative engines (ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot) assess brand authority through **co-citation and entity consensus across independent, authoritative domains**.

BharatYatra operates under strict ethical guidelines:
1. **Zero Link Buying / PBNs:** Never purchase sponsored links, private blog network articles, or black-hat directory placements. These violate Google Webmaster Guidelines and trigger LLM hallucination and spam penalties.
2. **Zero Fabricated Reviews:** All reviews, ratings, and testimonials must originate from genuine registered users with verified booking references or contribution IDs.
3. **Information-First Editorial Value:** Outreach pitches must provide genuine data, unique research, cultural insights, or functional technology value that editors and journalists want to reference naturally.
4. **No Unilateral Outreach Without Authorization:** No external emails, PR releases, or messages will be dispatched without explicit executive sign-off.
