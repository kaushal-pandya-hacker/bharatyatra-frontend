# Customer Support Ticketing Workflows v1.0

## 1. Ticket States
`OPEN` $\rightarrow$ `ASSIGNED` $\rightarrow$ `IN_PROGRESS` $\rightarrow$ `WAITING_FOR_USER` / `WAITING_FOR_SUPPLIER` $\rightarrow$ `RESOLVED` $\rightarrow$ `CLOSED`.

## 2. Internal Agent Notes
- Agents can record internal notes (`internalNotes`) for operational context. Internal notes are strictly hidden from customers and suppliers.
