## 1. Safety Guardrail Audit

- **Zero Unapproved Financial Charges**: Adaptive AI suggestions involving additional costs or paid booking modifications require explicit user 1-click approval. Auto-apply NEVER triggers financial transactions.
- **Immutable Itinerary Versioning**: Every approved adaptation spawns a new immutable itinerary version (`v1` → `v2`). Historical versions remain auditable and rollback-ready.
- **Cascading Delay Detection**: Ingested bus delays automatically evaluate check-in timing and downstream activity slots.
