# Gujarat Market Analysis — Chalo Farva v1.1

**Market Dynamics & Travel Intent**:
- **Top 5 Intent Hubs**:
  1. Statue of Unity (Ekta Nagar)
  2. Kutch (Rann Utsav)
  3. Dwarka & Somnath (Pilgrimage Circuit)
  4. Gir National Park (Wildlife Safari)
  5. Diu & Mandvi (Beach & Heritage)
- **Seasonality Pattern**: High demand peaks in October–March (Festive + Winter season). Monsoon demand concentrates in Saputara and Polo Forest.
